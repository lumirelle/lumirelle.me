It was my first time riding a roller coaster. I had never tried anything so exciting and terrifying 😱.

When you sit down and then the coaster 🎢 starts, you never know what will happen next. The coaster just speeds up suddenly, and the rail goes up and down, left and right, lets riders go upside down, and even makes you feel like flying in the air and then crashing into the earth's core. This feeling of weightlessness and helplessness is definitely terrifying. I felt like my heart was going to jump out of my chest.

The whole process can be very short, but it feels like a lifetime. The first part made me feel like I was going to die, and it almost broke me down in the last part, but when it ended peacefully, I had never felt so brave and excited in my life. After this experience, I feel that I have finally taken the first step towards growth.

Just like in Yu Hua's books, _I'm as timid as a mouse_ 🐁. All my courage comes from doing something that goes "beyond" the bounds, such as staying up later, procrastinating until the deadline, or playing video games and letting the characters do what I dare not do. Like Bronya Rand in _Honkai: Star Rail_, the most outrageous thing we have ever done is to say "I will, on behalf of the will of the architects, thrust this spear into your nose".

To tell the truth, I have never been so clearly aware that the trembling and anxiety when facing a feat is not cowardice, but a normal physiological reaction. It is only when one is afraid of facing fear itself that one can be considered cowardly.

Now, I take the first step 👣 to face my fears, and I gain the initial courage 🥊 to take more steps in the future. Just take care of this courage, let it grow healthily, and it will definitely become a strong power that can support me in facing more challenges in the future.
